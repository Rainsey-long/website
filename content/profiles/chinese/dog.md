## Personality

Some friends stay. Through good years and hard ones, they show up. The Dog is that kind of friend.

If you were born in a Dog year, you may value loyalty above almost everything. You tend to stand by the people you love. When someone needs help, you are often among the first to offer it.

Dogs are usually honest and fair. You may have a strong sense of right and wrong. Unfairness, even toward a stranger, can stir you to speak up.

You can also be thoughtful and a little cautious. You like to understand people before you fully trust them. Once you do, your trust runs deep. Your care can make others feel truly safe.

There is often a quiet, watchful side to the Dog as well. You may notice when a friend is not quite themselves, or when a group is drifting apart. You tend to respond with practical help rather than big words, and that steady presence is deeply valued.

## Strengths

- Loyalty that lasts
- Honesty and integrity
- A strong sense of fairness
- Protectiveness toward loved ones
- Dependability in a crisis
- Kindness without show

Your faithfulness is a rare gift. People often know they can count on you. Your honesty can help others feel grounded, and your sense of fairness can bring calm to a tense situation.

## Challenges

- Worrying about what could go wrong
- Caution that delays decisions
- Seeing things as strictly right or wrong
- Taking criticism to heart
- Carrying others' burdens alone

Your caring nature is lovely, yet it can lead you to worry for everyone. It may help to notice which concerns you can act on and which you can set down. You might also allow some grey areas. People and situations are often more complex than they first appear.

## In love

The Dog loves with devotion and sincerity. You tend to be a steady, protective partner. You show care through presence, honesty and small acts of support.

You usually look for someone you can trust completely. Shared values matter to you. Once you commit, you tend to be deeply faithful.

The Tiger and the Horse are traditional best matches. Both share your loyalty and enjoy an active, honest life. The Rabbit is another strong pairing. Its gentle warmth fits well with your protective care.

The Dragon and the Rooster are seen as more challenging matches. The Dragon's grand ambitions may feel unrealistic to you, and the Rooster's sharp critiques can sting. Honest talk and mutual respect can help these pairings find their footing.

## At work

At work, Dogs often thrive in roles with purpose. You may enjoy healthcare support, teaching, social work, law, public service, security or any field where you can help or protect others.

You tend to be reliable and conscientious. You work well when you believe in what you are doing. A workplace that feels dishonest or unfair can trouble you.

Colleagues often trust you deeply. You might help yourself by sharing your own needs and ideas more openly. Your voice is valuable, not only your support.

## The five element types

Wood Dogs (1934, 1994) are warm and cooperative. They tend to enjoy teamwork and helping their community. Their loyalty often comes with an easy friendliness.

Fire Dogs (1946, 2006) are energetic and bold. They often stand up strongly for what they believe in. Learning to rest can help them sustain their passion.

Earth Dogs (1958, 2018) are steady and practical. They tend to value home, fairness and routine. Others often rely on their calm and patience.

Metal Dogs (1970, 2030) are especially principled and determined. They often hold firm standards. A little flexibility can help them relax and connect.

Water Dogs (1922, 1982) are intuitive and gentle. They often understand others' feelings well. They may adapt more easily than other Dogs and bring calm to a group.

## Lucky elements

These traditional associations are offered as folklore and fun. Lucky colours for the Dog are often listed as red, green and purple. Lucky numbers are commonly 3, 4 and 9.

You might enjoy wearing one of these colours on a day spent with people you love. For a Dog, though, the best fortune usually comes from the trust you give and the trust you earn in return.
