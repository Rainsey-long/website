## Personality

The Dragon is the only mythical creature in the zodiac, and it often feels that way. There is something larger than life about this sign. Bright colour, big sky, a sense of possibility.

If you were born in a Dragon year, you may carry a natural confidence. You tend to believe things can be done, and that belief can make them happen. You often think in big pictures rather than small steps.

Dragons are usually generous with their energy. You might be the one who gets a group excited or pushes a project past its first hurdle. People often look to you when they need someone to lead.

There is also a soft side that not everyone sees. Dragons care deeply about being understood and respected. When you feel truly seen, you can be one of the warmest and most loyal friends anyone could have.

## Strengths

- Confidence and a strong presence
- Big, imaginative thinking
- Enthusiasm that inspires others
- Courage to take the lead
- Generosity with time and energy
- Resilience after setbacks

Your vision can be a guiding light for others. When you commit to a goal, you bring real force behind it. People often feel braver simply by being near your energy.

## Challenges

- Impatience with small details
- High expectations of yourself and others
- Difficulty hearing criticism
- Taking on too many projects at once
- Pride that makes it hard to ask for help

Your ambition is a gift, yet it can make ordinary days feel too small. It may help to celebrate modest wins, not only the big ones. You might also try inviting feedback early. A good idea often grows stronger when others help shape it.

## In love

The Dragon loves with passion and generosity. You tend to bring excitement and warmth to a relationship. Grand gestures may come naturally to you.

You often look for a partner who can keep up with you and who also admires your spirit. Feeling respected matters a great deal. You may also need someone who can gently ground you when your plans grow very large.

The Rat and the Monkey are traditional best matches. Both share your quick pace and love of possibility. The Rooster is another strong pairing. Its careful planning and honesty can turn your dreams into real steps.

The Dog and the Rabbit are seen as more challenging matches. The Dog's cautious realism may feel like doubt, and the Rabbit's preference for calm can clash with your energy. Patience and listening can help these bonds grow.

## At work

At work, Dragons often thrive in roles with scope and visibility. You may enjoy leadership, creative direction, entrepreneurship, public speaking or any field where vision matters.

You tend to do well when given freedom to shape the direction of a project. Micromanagement can frustrate you.

Colleagues often feel energised by you. You might help your ideas succeed by working closely with people who love detail. Together, you can build things that last.

## The five element types

Wood Dragons (1964, 2024) are creative and open-minded. They tend to share ideas freely and enjoy working with others. Their imagination often has a practical side.

Fire Dragons (1976, 2036) are the most passionate of all. They are often bold, expressive and full of drive. Learning to rest can help them keep their fire burning steadily.

Earth Dragons (1928, 1988) are more grounded and patient. They tend to think things through and value lasting results. Their steady confidence often earns deep trust.

Metal Dragons (1940, 2000) are strong-willed and determined. They hold firm standards and pursue goals with focus. Softening their approach can help them bring others along.

Water Dragons (1952, 2012) are calm and perceptive. They often read situations well and adapt with ease. Their big ideas come with a thoughtful, steady touch.

## Lucky elements

These traditional associations are offered as folklore and fun. Lucky colours for the Dragon are often listed as gold, silver and grey. Lucky numbers are commonly 1, 6 and 7.

You might enjoy a touch of gold or silver on a day when you want to feel confident. Still, a Dragon's best luck tends to come from the courage to begin and the warmth to bring others along.
