## Personality

A long table, plenty of food and room for one more guest. That is the Pig's idea of a good evening. As the last sign in the cycle, the Pig gathers everyone in.

If you were born in a Pig year, you may have a generous heart. You tend to share what you have, whether that is time, a meal or a listening ear. Making others comfortable often brings you real joy.

Pigs are usually sincere. You say what you feel and mean what you say. You may find it hard to understand people who play games or hide their intentions.

You also know how to enjoy life. Good food, good company, music, nature, a slow afternoon. You tend to appreciate simple pleasures, and that can remind others to slow down too.

## Strengths

- Generosity and hospitality
- Sincerity and honesty
- Tolerance and forgiveness
- Warmth that puts people at ease
- Patience and good humour
- Diligence once committed

Your kindness can create a real sense of belonging. People often feel welcome around you. Your forgiving nature helps relationships heal, and your steady effort often brings quiet success.

## Challenges

- Trusting too quickly
- Difficulty saying no
- Avoiding conflict until it grows
- Indulging comfort over effort at times
- Putting others first too often

Your open heart is a gift, yet it can leave you exposed. It may help to take a little more time before trusting new people fully. You might also practise saying no kindly. Protecting your own energy lets you keep giving in the ways that matter most.

## In love

The Pig loves with warmth, sincerity and generosity. You tend to make a partner feel cherished and welcome. Shared meals, cosy evenings and small kindnesses may be how you show care.

You often look for a partner who is honest and affectionate. Trust is important to you. When you feel secure, you are usually loyal, playful and deeply devoted.

The Rabbit and the Goat are traditional best matches. Both share your love of comfort, kindness and peace. The Tiger is another strong pairing. Its courage can encourage you, and your gentleness can help it feel at home.

The Snake and the Monkey are seen as more challenging matches. The Snake's guarded nature may feel hard to read, and the Monkey's quick changes can unsettle your trusting style. Clear, kind communication can help these bonds grow.

## At work

At work, Pigs often thrive in roles that involve people and care. You may enjoy hospitality, food, healthcare support, teaching, charity work, crafts or any field where generosity is valued.

You tend to be diligent once you believe in a task. A friendly, cooperative workplace helps you do your best. Harsh competition or office politics can wear you down.

Colleagues often appreciate your good nature and reliability. You might help yourself by speaking up for your own ideas and boundaries. Your kindness is a strength, and so is your voice.

## The five element types

Wood Pigs (1935, 1995) are friendly and community-minded. They tend to enjoy teamwork and helping others grow. Their generosity often has a practical side.

Fire Pigs (1947, 2007) are passionate and lively. They often pursue what they love with real energy. Balancing enthusiasm with rest can help them thrive.

Earth Pigs (1959, 2019) are steady and nurturing. They tend to value home, family and comfort. Others often feel calm and secure around them.

Metal Pigs (1971, 2031) are more determined than many Pigs. They often hold firm values and follow through on promises. A gentle approach helps them connect with others.

Water Pigs (1923, 1983) are intuitive and empathetic. They often understand others' feelings deeply. They may enjoy creative or caring work that draws on that insight.

## Lucky elements

These traditional associations are shared as folklore and a bit of fun. Lucky colours for the Pig are often listed as yellow, grey, brown and gold. Lucky numbers are commonly 2, 5 and 8.

You might enjoy a warm golden tone at a gathering with friends. For a Pig, though, the best luck often comes from an open heart, a full table and the people who gather around it.
