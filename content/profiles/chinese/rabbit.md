## Personality

Think of a quiet garden in the early evening. Soft light, a gentle breeze, everything in its place. The Rabbit carries that kind of calm with it.

If you were born in a Rabbit year, you may have a gift for making people feel at ease. You notice when someone is uncomfortable. You often know the right word to soften a hard moment.

Rabbits tend to love beauty and comfort. You might care about how a room feels, how a meal is presented or how a message is phrased. These small touches matter to you, and they make life better for others too.

You are not one to seek conflict. You would rather find a peaceful path. This can be mistaken for shyness, yet there is real strength here. When something truly matters, you can be quietly firm.

## Strengths

- Kindness and emotional awareness
- Tact in delicate situations
- A refined sense of style and comfort
- Careful, thoughtful decisions
- Loyalty to close friends
- Calm that steadies others

Your gentle approach can open doors that force would close. People often trust you because you listen well and do not rush to judge. You may be the friend others call when they need a kind ear.

## Challenges

- Avoiding hard conversations for too long
- Worrying about others' opinions
- Hesitating when a choice feels risky
- Retreating when feeling overwhelmed
- Putting others' comfort before your own needs

Your wish for harmony is lovely, yet it can mean small problems grow quietly. It may help to speak up early, while things are still easy to fix. You might also remind yourself that your needs count too. A calm, honest word is often enough.

## In love

The Rabbit loves with tenderness and care. You tend to create a warm, safe space for a partner. Thoughtful gestures and gentle attention are how you show affection.

You may move slowly at first. You like to feel secure before you open up fully. Once you do, you are usually devoted and attentive.

The Goat and the Pig are traditional best matches. Both share your love of comfort, kindness and a peaceful home. The Dog is another strong pairing. Its loyalty and honesty can help you feel truly safe.

The Rooster and the Dragon are seen as more challenging matches. The Rooster's directness may feel sharp to you, and the Dragon's big energy can feel overwhelming. With patience and clear communication, these pairings can still find common ground.

## At work

At work, Rabbits often do well in calm, considered settings. You may enjoy roles involving design, writing, counselling, diplomacy, hospitality or anything that rewards care and good taste.

You tend to work steadily and thoughtfully. You like to get details right. Loud, high-pressure environments may wear you down over time.

Colleagues often value your ability to smooth tensions and find agreement. You might help yourself by sharing your ideas with more confidence. Your quiet insights can be some of the most useful in the room.

## The five element types

Wood Rabbits (1915, 1975) are generous and cooperative. They tend to work well with others and enjoy helping. Their kindness often draws people close.

Fire Rabbits (1927, 1987) bring more warmth and expression than most Rabbits. They can be lively and creative. They may need quiet time to recharge after busy days.

Earth Rabbits (1939, 1999) are grounded and practical. They tend to value security and steady routines. Their calm judgement is often trusted by others.

Metal Rabbits (1951, 2011) are more determined than their gentle reputation suggests. They often have clear standards and quiet ambition. A little flexibility can help them relax.

Water Rabbits (1963, 2023) are intuitive and deeply sensitive. They often understand feelings without words. They may thrive in peaceful, creative settings.

## Lucky elements

These traditional associations are shared as folklore and a little fun. Lucky colours for the Rabbit are often listed as red, pink, purple and blue. Lucky numbers are commonly 3, 4 and 6.

You might enjoy choosing a soft shade of one of these colours for a calm day. For a Rabbit, though, the luckiest thing is often the peace you create around you, and the trust that grows from it.
