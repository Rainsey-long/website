## Personality

Morning starts with the Rooster. There is something bright and wide awake about this sign. Clear eyes, a clear voice and a clear plan for the day.

If you were born in a Rooster year, you may notice details others overlook. A missing step. A crooked line. A small error in the numbers. You tend to see what needs fixing, and you often know how to fix it.

Roosters are usually honest and direct. You say what you mean. Many people value this because they never have to guess what you think.

You also have a proud, confident side. You may enjoy looking your best and doing your best. Underneath that polish there is often a hard worker who cares deeply about getting things right.

Roosters often like a tidy rhythm to the day. A clear list, an early start and a sense of order can help you feel at your best. When life gets busy, that structure is often what keeps you and the people around you steady.

## Strengths

- Honesty and straightforward speech
- Strong attention to detail
- Organisation and planning
- Hard work and dedication
- Confidence and self-respect
- Reliability with commitments

Your careful eye can save a project from small mistakes that grow large. People often trust you to keep things on track. Your honesty can be a steady guide when others are unsure.

## Challenges

- Criticism that can feel too sharp
- Perfectionism that adds pressure
- Difficulty letting small things go
- Impatience with disorder
- Pride that resists apology

Your high standards are a strength, yet they can sometimes weigh on you and others. It may help to choose which details truly matter today. You might also soften feedback with a word of warmth. People often hear honest advice best when it comes with kindness.

## In love

The Rooster loves with loyalty and care. You tend to show affection through practical support and steady commitment. You remember what matters and make sure it gets done.

You often look for a partner who is honest and dependable. Shared values matter a great deal to you. When you trust someone, you are usually faithful and protective.

The Ox and the Snake are traditional best matches. Both share your love of order, depth and steady effort. The Dragon is another strong pairing. Its bold vision can inspire you, and your planning can help its dreams take shape.

The Rabbit and the Dog are seen as more challenging matches. The Rabbit's gentle sensitivity may feel bruised by your directness, and the Dog's own strong principles can clash with yours. With patience and soft words, these bonds can still flourish.

## At work

At work, Roosters often shine in roles that reward precision and organisation. You may enjoy administration, accounting, editing, science, events, management or any field where accuracy matters.

You tend to plan well and work steadily. Clear structure helps you thrive. A chaotic workplace with unclear goals can frustrate you.

Colleagues often rely on your sharp eye. You might help yourself by praising others' efforts as readily as you spot their errors. A balance of both can make you a trusted leader.

## The five element types

Wood Roosters (1945, 2005) are cooperative and fair-minded. They tend to enjoy working toward shared goals. Their honesty often comes with generosity.

Fire Roosters (1957, 2017) are energetic and confident. They often lead with passion and strong opinions. A little patience can help them bring others along.

Earth Roosters (1969, 2029) are grounded and practical. They tend to value steady progress and careful planning. Their reliability is often unmatched.

Metal Roosters (1921, 1981) are especially precise and determined. They often hold very high standards. Allowing some flexibility can ease their own pressure.

Water Roosters (1933, 1993) are thoughtful and articulate. They often communicate well and adapt with ease. Their sharp minds come with real perceptiveness.

## Lucky elements

These traditional associations are shared as folklore and a bit of fun. Lucky colours for the Rooster are often listed as gold, brown and yellow. Lucky numbers are commonly 5, 7 and 8.

You might enjoy a warm golden touch on a day when you want to feel organised and bright. For a Rooster, though, the best luck often comes from honest effort, careful plans and a kind word along the way.
