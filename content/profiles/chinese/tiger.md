## Personality

The Tiger walks into a room and the air seems to change. There is energy, presence and a certain spark. People notice.

If you were born in a Tiger year, you may feel drawn to challenge. You like to test yourself. When something seems unfair, you are often the first to speak. Courage tends to come naturally to you.

Yet the Tiger is not only bold. There is a big heart under the stripes. You can be fiercely protective of people you love. You often give generously, whether that means time, attention or encouragement.

Tigers also love freedom. You may feel restless if life becomes too routine. New places, new ideas and fresh starts can bring out your best. You tend to follow your instincts, and they often serve you well.

## Strengths

- Courage in difficult moments
- Generosity and a warm heart
- Natural confidence and presence
- Strong sense of justice
- Enthusiasm that lifts others
- Willingness to try something new

Your energy can be contagious. When you believe in something, others often start to believe too. You are good at beginnings, at rallying people and at taking the first brave step.

## Challenges

- Acting quickly before thinking it through
- Impatience with slower people or plans
- Strong reactions when feeling challenged
- Difficulty accepting limits or rules
- Taking on too much at once

Your speed is a strength, yet it can also lead you into tangles. A short pause before a big decision can help. You might also notice when a strong feeling is asking for space, not action. Learning to wait a little can make your courage even more effective.

## In love

The Tiger loves with passion and openness. When you care about someone, they usually know it. You can be romantic, playful and deeply loyal.

You tend to need a partner who respects your independence. Feeling hemmed in can make you restless. A relationship that offers both closeness and room to roam is often where you thrive.

The Horse and the Dog are traditional best matches. The Horse shares your love of adventure. The Dog shares your loyalty and sense of fairness. The Pig is another warm pairing. Its easy kindness can calm your fire and help you feel at home.

The Monkey and the Snake are seen as more challenging matches. The Monkey's playful tricks may test your patience, and the Snake's quiet caution can feel hard to reach. Honest conversations and mutual respect can help bridge these differences.

## At work

At work, the Tiger is often a natural leader. You may enjoy roles where you can take charge, start new projects or stand up for a cause. Advocacy, entrepreneurship, teaching, performance, coaching or fields with real movement can suit you.

You tend to do your best work when you believe in the mission. Routine tasks with no clear purpose can drain your enthusiasm.

Colleagues often admire your courage. You might help yourself by inviting quieter voices into decisions. Pairing your drive with others' careful planning can make your ideas go further.

## The five element types

Wood Tigers (1914, 1974) are friendly and open-minded. They tend to work well with others and enjoy growth. Their warmth makes them good team leaders.

Fire Tigers (1926, 1986) are full of passion and spark. They are often charismatic and eager for adventure. Steady habits can help them sustain their bright energy.

Earth Tigers (1938, 1998) are more grounded than most Tigers. They tend to think before acting and value stability. Their courage often comes with good judgement.

Metal Tigers (1950, 2010) are strong-willed and determined. They set clear goals and pursue them with force. A little flexibility can soften their edges.

Water Tigers (1962, 2022) are perceptive and calm. They often read people well and stay open to new ideas. Their brave hearts are paired with real thoughtfulness.

## Lucky elements

These traditional associations are offered as folklore and fun. Lucky colours for the Tiger are often listed as blue, grey and orange. Lucky numbers are commonly 1, 3 and 4.

You might enjoy a splash of one of these colours on a day when you want to feel bold. Still, a Tiger's best fortune usually comes from courage, kindness and the willingness to take that first step.
