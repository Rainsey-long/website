## Personality

Aquarius is often pictured as the water-bearer, pouring water out for everyone. Despite the image, it is an air sign. The water stands for ideas and knowledge, shared freely with the wider world.

As an air sign, Aquarius lives in thought and conversation. It is a fixed sign, which means its views, once formed, can be steady and strong. Its modern ruler is Uranus, linked with surprise and invention. Its traditional ruler is Saturn, linked with structure. That mix can produce someone who thinks freely but builds carefully.

If you are an Aquarius, you may feel a little different from the crowd, and you may be fine with that. You often ask "why not?" when others ask "why?".

You tend to care about people in general, not just those close to you. Fairness, community and progress may matter a lot. You might volunteer, sign petitions or simply treat everyone you meet as an equal.

You also enjoy friendship. Many Aquarians are easy to talk to and full of interesting ideas.

## Strengths

- You think in original, inventive ways.
- You treat people as equals.
- You care about fairness and community.
- You stay calm and rational when others panic.
- You are a loyal and open-minded friend.

These qualities make Aquarius a natural innovator and a good friend to many. You can help groups imagine something better than what already exists.

## Challenges

- Close emotional moments may feel awkward at times.
- Once you have decided, changing your mind can be hard.
- You might seem detached when you are simply thinking.
- Rules that seem pointless may frustrate you.
- You may value your freedom so highly that others feel shut out.

It may help to show your warmth in words as well as ideas. Letting people see how you feel, not only what you think, can deepen your bonds. Being independent and being close are not opposites. You can enjoy both.

## In love

In love, Aquarius often begins with friendship. You may be drawn to someone because of a great conversation or a shared cause. Mental connection can matter as much as anything else.

You value a partner who respects your independence. You tend to give the same freedom in return. Jealousy and tight control rarely sit well with you.

Air signs Gemini and Libra often match your love of ideas and talk. Fire signs Aries and Sagittarius can bring energy and adventure that you enjoy. Leo, your opposite sign, can feel magnetic. Leo is warm and personal while you are cool and wide-ranging, and each can teach the other something.

Taurus and Scorpio sit three signs away. With them there can be creative tension. All three of you are fixed signs, so compromise can take practice. With patience and humour, these pairings can be lasting.

You may be happiest with a partner who is also your best friend and who enjoys your quirks.

## At work

At work, Aquarius often brings fresh thinking. Technology, science, design, social causes, research and anything that involves solving new problems can suit you.

You tend to work well with some freedom. Strict hierarchies or outdated rules may test your patience. Give you a challenge and some trust, and you may come up with something no one expected.

You can be a good team member when the team shares a purpose. You may also be the person who suggests a better system, and then helps everyone adopt it.

## Friends and family

As a friend, Aquarius is open, loyal and interesting. You might have friends from many walks of life. You enjoy groups, clubs and communities with a shared interest.

You can be the friend who encourages others to be themselves. People often feel accepted around you.

In families, Aquarius may be the one with unusual hobbies or new ideas. You might introduce relatives to new technology or new ways of thinking. You may also need some time alone to recharge, even at busy family gatherings.

## Lucky elements

Saturday is traditionally linked with Aquarius through Saturn, the sign's traditional ruler. Electric blue and silver are colours often associated with Aquarius, bright and modern like a clear winter sky. The numbers 4 and 7 are among its customary lucky numbers. Four is often linked with Uranus in modern number lore, and seven with insight. These are fun, traditional touches rather than predictions.
