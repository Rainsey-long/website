## Personality

Cancer begins at midsummer in the northern hemisphere, when the days are at their longest. Yet the sign itself is often linked with evenings, kitchens and lamplight. It is the sign of home.

Cancer is a water sign, so feelings run deep and intuition is strong. It is a cardinal sign, which gives it a quiet ability to start things, especially things that bring people together. Its ruler is the Moon. Like the Moon, your moods may wax and wane, and you may feel the pull of the past.

Your symbol is the crab. It carries a shell, and so do you. With strangers you might seem reserved. With people you trust, you can be wonderfully warm, funny and open.

You tend to remember things. A kind word from years ago, an old song, the smell of a grandparent's cooking. Memory and belonging matter to you.

You are also more resourceful than people sometimes assume. When someone you love needs protecting, you can be surprisingly firm.

## Strengths

- You sense what others are feeling.
- You make people feel welcome and looked after.
- Your loyalty is steady and long-lasting.
- You have a rich imagination.
- You are quietly determined when it counts.
- You keep family stories and traditions alive.

These gifts make Cancer a natural carer, host and keeper of connections. People often feel safer simply because you are around.

## Challenges

- You may take things to heart more than intended.
- Old hurts can linger longer than you would like.
- Your shell can keep good people out as well as bad.
- You might give so much that you forget your own needs.
- Moods can shift with little warning.

It may help to speak your feelings gently and early, instead of waiting for others to guess. Making time for your own rest is not selfish. It lets you keep caring for others well. Letting someone care for you in return can feel unusual, and it can also be lovely.

## In love

In love, Cancer is tender, attentive and committed. You want to feel safe, and you want your partner to feel safe too. Small rituals, such as a shared breakfast or a regular phone call, can mean a great deal.

You may take some time before you fully trust someone. Once you do, you give your heart completely. You remember anniversaries and notice when your partner seems tired.

Water signs Scorpio and Pisces often meet you on the same emotional wavelength. Earth signs Taurus and Virgo can offer the stability that helps you relax. Capricorn, your opposite sign, can feel magnetic. Capricorn builds structure in the outer world while you build warmth at home, and together that can feel complete.

Aries and Libra sit three signs away. With them there can be creative tension. Aries moves fast, and Libra seeks harmony through talk rather than feeling. With patience, both can help you grow.

You may do best with a partner who is gentle with your feelings and who sees home as something you build together.

## At work

At work, Cancer often brings care and intuition. You may notice when a colleague is struggling, or when a customer needs reassurance. Roles in caring, teaching, hospitality, food, design for the home or anything with a sense of service can suit you.

You tend to work well in a team that feels like a small family. A cold or competitive office may drain you. When you feel valued, your loyalty and effort can be remarkable.

You may also have a good instinct for resources and planning ahead. Many Cancers like to have a little set aside, just in case.

## Friends and family

As a friend, Cancer is the one with tissues, tea and time. You listen well and you remember what matters to people. Your friends may think of your home as a place they can always drop by.

You often have a small, close circle rather than a large crowd. Friendships that began long ago may still be some of your favourites.

Family is often central for Cancer. You might be the one who hosts holidays, keeps photo albums or checks in on older relatives. Your family may not always say it, but they often feel held together by your care.

## Lucky elements

Monday is the lucky day traditionally linked with Cancer, because it is named after the Moon. Silver and white are its customary colours, reflecting moonlight on water. The numbers 2 and 7 are often called its lucky numbers. Two suggests partnership and closeness, and seven has long been associated with the Moon's cycles. These links are part of astrology's tradition, shared here for enjoyment rather than as a forecast.
