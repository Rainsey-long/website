## Personality

Libra begins near the autumn equinox, when day and night are almost equal in length. That moment of balance is a fitting start for a sign whose symbol is the scales.

Libra is an air sign, so it lives through ideas, words and relationships. It is a cardinal sign, which means it likes to set things in motion, especially social things. Venus rules Libra. In Taurus, Venus leans toward comfort. In Libra, it leans toward grace, harmony and partnership.

If you are a Libra, you probably care about fairness. Unkindness or injustice can bother you deeply. You often want everyone to feel heard.

You may also have a strong sense of style. A well-set table, a pleasing colour combination or a thoughtful arrangement of furniture can lift your mood. Beauty is not a luxury to you. It is part of how life feels right.

Socially, you tend to be easy company. You know how to make someone feel at ease, and you can read a room well.

## Strengths

- You see both sides of a disagreement.
- You bring people together with tact.
- Your taste and sense of style are often admired.
- You are warm, polite and easy to talk to.
- You care about fairness for everyone.
- You collaborate well.

These strengths make Libra a gifted mediator and a lovely host. Groups often feel calmer and kinder when you are there.

## Challenges

- Decisions can take you a long time.
- You may avoid conflict even when it is needed.
- Pleasing others can come before your own wishes.
- You might hide your true opinion to keep the peace.
- Being alone for long can feel uncomfortable.

It may help to remember that honest disagreement can strengthen a relationship. Your own preferences count too. Trying smaller decisions quickly, without weighing every option, can build confidence for the bigger ones.

## In love

Libra is often called the sign of partnership, and love matters a great deal to you. You enjoy romance, shared plans and the feeling of being a team.

You tend to be attentive and considerate. You may plan lovely dates, notice what your partner enjoys and try hard to keep the relationship harmonious.

Air signs Gemini and Aquarius often match your love of conversation and ideas. Fire signs Leo and Sagittarius can bring warmth and adventure that you enjoy. Aries, your opposite sign, can feel magnetic. Aries is direct where you are diplomatic. Each can help the other find a better balance between self and partner.

Cancer and Capricorn sit three signs away. With them there may be creative tension. Cancer leads with feelings, and Capricorn with duty, while you lead with fairness. Talking openly can help these bonds grow.

You may feel happiest with a partner who values kindness and who encourages you to speak your mind.

## At work

At work, Libra often shines in roles that involve people. Design, law, diplomacy, human resources, counselling, events and the arts can all suit your talents.

You tend to work well in teams. You can help colleagues understand each other and find solutions everyone can live with.

A pleasant, well-designed workspace may matter to you. Harsh environments or constant conflict can drain your energy. When the atmosphere is good, you can be a calm and creative presence.

It can help to give yourself deadlines for decisions. That way, your careful weighing of options leads to action.

## Friends and family

As a friend, Libra is gracious and fun. You enjoy dinners, outings and gatherings where everyone gets along. You often remember to include the person standing at the edge of the group.

You may be the friend others ask for advice about relationships or style. You can give balanced, gentle feedback.

In families, Libra is often the peacemaker. When siblings argue, you may be the one who helps them see each other's point of view. You might also be the relative who makes holidays look and feel beautiful.

## Lucky elements

Friday is the traditional lucky day for Libra, since it is linked with Venus, the sign's ruler. Pastel blue and rose are colours often associated with Libra, soft and balanced like a calm autumn sky. The numbers 6 and 15 are among its customary lucky numbers. Six has old ties to Venus and harmony, and fifteen adds up to six. These are traditional, playful details, not forecasts.
